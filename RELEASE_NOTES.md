First public edition of Rafael Ode Brino's HMM implementation and academic intrusion-detection study.

- Categorical-HMM forward/backward, filtering, posterior probabilities, unconstrained EM, and Viterbi.
- Independent exhaustive-oracle tests, numerical edge-case checks, and a seeded example.
- Supplied six-page report and study modules, with corrected sole-author attribution and documented experimental boundaries.

The software is MIT licensed. The attached report has separate copyright terms in `NOTICE.md`. Reported IDS metrics are historical study results; this release's generated-data checks do not reproduce the external-data experiment.
